## ✨ What is EHSAAN COLOUR?

EHSAAN COLOUR is a simple, visually focused space for exploring colours and finding inspiration for digital designs.

### 🎨 Features

- 🌈 **Explore Colour Palettes** — Discover beautiful colour combinations.
- 🖼️ **Design Inspiration** — Find colours for interfaces, graphics, wallpapers, and creative projects.
- 🔢 **HEX Codes** — Explore and copy HEX colour codes for easy use in your designs.
- 💜 **Minimal Interface** — Enjoy a clean, distraction-free experience.
- 📱 **Accessible Anywhere** — Explore colours through the web app.

---

## 🎨 Design Philosophy

EHSAAN COLOUR follows the EHSAAN design philosophy:

> **Make useful things. Make them feel good to use.**

- Clean layouts and comfortable spacing
- Beautiful colour-focused visuals
- Simple navigation
- Minimal distractions

**Less complexity. More creativity.**
